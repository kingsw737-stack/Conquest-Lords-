ARCANE WARFARE

Turn-based wizard artillery. Blast the terrain, read the wind, be the last mage standing.

iPhone tip: Safari can't go fullscreen. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the game from the icon for true fullscreen. Turn your phone sideways to play.

How to play

Take turns casting spells across the battlefield. Craters tear holes in the ground, wind bends every shot, and the water is deadly. The last mage standing wins.
